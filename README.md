# UAETales

**Every Tale. Every Emirate.**

UAETales is a digital magazine covering the United Arab Emirates — stories, guides and news about life, culture, travel, food and events across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.
